# Documents Folder

This folder is for storing your PDF documents that will be available for download on your website.

## Upload Your PDFs Here

Place your PDF files in this folder with descriptive names:

### Suggested Files:
1. `leadership-ai-era.pdf` - Your leadership and AI document
2. `neuroleadership-guide.pdf` - Your neuroscience-based leadership guide
3. `transformation-framework.pdf` - Your organizational transformation framework

## After Uploading

Once you've uploaded your PDFs here, update the links in:
- `index.html` (English version)
- `index-es.html` (Spanish version)

Change the download links from:
```html
href="#"
```

To:
```html
href="documents/your-file-name.pdf"
```

See **PDF-UPLOAD-GUIDE.md** in the root folder for complete instructions.
